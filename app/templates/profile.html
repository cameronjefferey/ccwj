Broker sync (via SnapTrade)

Schwab, Fidelity, Vanguard, Robinhood, IBKR, Alpaca, Wealthsimple, and others through the SnapTrade Connection Portal. Connect once per broker; we refresh from then on. This is not a lifetime archive of the account.

What broker sync actually includes:
  • Within minutes: current positions, cash balance, and account total.
  • Within a few hours: recent trade history (sometimes up to 24h for the very first sync).
  • Not the life of the account. We request up to {{ snaptrade_full_history_lookback_days }} days; each broker only returns what it still has on file. Schwab typically sends about 1–2 years of trades, not 5 years and not the account open date. Daily values start the first day we captured a snapshot.
If trades aren't visible right away, sync again later — the broker still posts them on its own schedule. Years the aggregator never sent only arrive if you upload a CSV of older history.
