What broker sync includes
  • Within minutes: current positions, cash balance, and account total.
  • Within a few hours: recent trade history (sometimes up to 24h for the very first sync).
  • Not the life of the account. We request up to {{ snaptrade_full_history_lookback_days | history_window }}; each broker only returns what it still has on file. Schwab typically sends about 1–2 years of trades, not 5 years and not the account open date. Daily values start the first day we captured a snapshot.

If trades aren't visible right away, sync again later — the broker still posts them on its own schedule. Years the aggregator never sent only arrive if you upload a CSV of older history. Already on Schwab (or any connected broker)? Connect again and pick the extra account in the portal — another household Schwab, a second IBKR login, a TD RRSP. Same broker is fine.
