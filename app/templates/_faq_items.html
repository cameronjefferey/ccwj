How far back does broker sync go?

Not the life of the account, and not 5 years. We request the longest window the aggregator allows (up to 5 years); each broker only returns what it still has on file. For Schwab that is typically about 1–2 years of trades. Daily account-value charts start even later — the first day we captured an end-of-day balance, which is after you connected, not when the account opened.

That gap changes how an account looks: P&L, positions, and the value chart only cover the window we actually received. To fill in older trades and cost basis, export from schwab.com (Accounts → History → Transactions, date range as far back as you can) and {% if current_user.is_authenticated %}upload the CSV{% else %}upload the CSV{% endif %}. A CSV cannot recreate daily balances before we started snapshotting.

Is my data secure?

Your trade data is processed and stored in Google BigQuery. Broker access goes through SnapTrade and is read-only. You sign in on your broker's page. HappyTrader does not store brokerage passwords. A CSV upload is optional, for older history the sync didn't include. Your HappyTrader password is hashed. Use a strong, unique one.

Can I have multiple accounts?

Yes. Connect each brokerage (or another account at the same broker) and we'll track them separately. You can filter by account on Positions, Strategies, and other views. Portfolio-wide totals are shown on Overview.

What strategies does HappyTrader recognize?

We auto-detect Covered Calls, Cash-Secured Puts, Wheels, Call Spreads, Put Spreads, Long Calls, Long Puts, Buy and Hold, and other option strategies. Classification is based on your trade patterns—no manual tagging.

What does AI Insights actually do?

AI Insights reads the same numbers we compute from your trades — especially exit timing and giveback vs daily option marks, DTE buckets, and strategy-level performance — and writes a plain-English read of those results. It surfaces patterns; it does not give financial advice.

You can also ask follow-up questions about your own trading patterns. Every answer is grounded in your data — observations only, not recommendations.

Flash and Haiku are included. Gemini 2.5 Pro, Claude Sonnet, and Claude Opus are part of the HappyTrader AI add-on.

What is the Strategy fit matrix?

A heatmap of every strategy you've used crossed against every sector you've traded. Each cell shows how your real trades performed — expectancy, win rate, and number of trades.

Each cell is the record for that pair: expectancy, win rate, and how many trades. Empty cells had no trades.

How do you tag sectors and subsectors?

Every ticker you trade is automatically tagged with its sector and subsector using public market data — no manual entry. Results roll up by sector on the Sectors page.
