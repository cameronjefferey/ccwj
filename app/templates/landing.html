How it works

Three steps, then the record

  1. 01

    Connect your brokerage

    Read-only, through SnapTrade. Schwab, Fidelity, Vanguard, Robinhood, and others. HappyTrader never stores your broker password.

  2. 02

    Strategies detected

    Covered calls, cash-secured puts, spreads, and the other labels are read from the fills and from the shares held when the option was written.

  3. 03

    See what's working

    Positions, strategies, and the fit matrix show where the P&L came from. The page is the record. It does not tell you what to trade next.
