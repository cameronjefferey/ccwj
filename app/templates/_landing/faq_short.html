Questions

What does it cost?

Lessons, replays, and paper trading are free, with no card. The 30-day trial starts only when you connect a real brokerage. After that the mirror stays readable and daily syncing pauses until you subscribe.

What brokers are supported?

Schwab, Fidelity, Vanguard, Robinhood, Interactive Brokers, and others, through SnapTrade. Access is read-only. HappyTrader never stores your broker password. Older history can be added from a CSV after you connect.

Is this investment advice?

No. HappyTrader is an educational record of trades you already made. It does not tell you what to trade next. {% include "_landing/disclaimer.html" %}
