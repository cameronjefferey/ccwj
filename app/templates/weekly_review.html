How this is computed

Rows come from the same fill feed as a past day's review (broker history dated {{ _tt_day.strftime('%a %b %-d') if _tt_day else 'the session' }}). This is not the weekly table below: a covered-call write on a stock you've held for months shows here even though that group didn't newly open this week. The dollar is realized G/L on that close — what you sold it for minus what you paid (equity: sale vs average cost; option: the contract's net of opens and closes). Opens and unmatched closes show an em dash, not cash in or out. A same-day close + open of the same call or put is one roll; the dollar is the closed leg's G/L, and a short roll meets the bar on a credit (or flat) or a strike that moved in the covered direction (calls up, puts down). Same-day trades appear after the next sync. Option expiry, assignment, and exercise are dated to the expiry session even when the broker posts them on Monday.
