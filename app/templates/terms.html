The service

HappyTrader is a trading mirror: analytics on the brokerage history you connect or upload. New accounts start a 30-day reverse trial (the clock starts when your first data lands). After that the pages stay readable; keeping the mirror live is a paid subscription. Numbers can still be wrong while we improve how we process trades. If something looks off, tell us.
