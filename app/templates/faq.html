Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I export my data from Schwab?

Trade History: Log in at schwab.com → Accounts → History → Transactions. Select your account, set the filter to All Transactions, pick a date range, and click Export to download the CSV.

Current Positions: Accounts → Positions → select your account → Export.

Both files are optional — history, current positions, or both. We support Schwab's web export format (not thinkorswim).

How far back does broker sync go?

Not the life of the account, and not 5 years. We request the longest window the aggregator allows (up to 1825 days); each broker only returns what it still has on file. For Schwab that is typically about 1–2 years of trades. Daily account-value charts start even later — the first day we captured an end-of-day balance, which is after you connected, not when the account opened.

That gap changes how an account looks: P&L, positions, and the value chart only cover the window we actually received. To fill in older trades and cost basis, export from schwab.com (Accounts → History → Transactions, date range as far back as you can) and upload the CSV. A CSV cannot recreate daily balances before we started snapshotting.

Is my data secure?

Your trade data is processed and stored in Google BigQuery. We do not store brokerage passwords or have API access to your account—you export and upload CSVs manually. Passwords are hashed. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your HappyTrader account.

What brokers are supported?

Right now CSV upload parses Charles Schwab's web export (schwab.com, not thinkorswim). You can connect other brokers (Fidelity, Vanguard, Robinhood, Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, Wealthsimple, and more) via SnapTrade. If we haven't fitted a given brokerage into our models yet, SnapTrade still connects it. The first handful of users get six months of Pro included free if they subscribe, while we calibrate.

Per-broker CSV export steps live on Upload — Schwab is ready; request any other brokerage from that page.

Do I have to re-upload CSVs every day?

No. Once you've done one upload to get started, you can connect your broker via SnapTrade from your Account page and we'll pull new transactions on a schedule — no daily export needed.

Broker sync is not a lifetime archive. If the aggregator didn't send years you still have in schwab.com, export those and upload the CSV once. We never see your broker password.

Can I have multiple accounts?

Yes. Upload data for each account and we'll track them separately. You can filter by account on Positions, Strategies, and other views. Portfolio-wide totals are shown on Overview.

What strategies does HappyTrader recognize?

We auto-detect Covered Calls, Cash-Secured Puts, Wheels, Call Spreads, Put Spreads, Long Calls, Long Puts, Buy and Hold, and other option strategies. Classification is based on your trade patterns—no manual tagging.

What does AI Insights actually do?

AI Insights reads the same numbers we compute from your trades — especially exit timing and giveback vs daily option marks, DTE buckets, and strategy-level performance — and writes a plain-English read of what's working and where you've left money on the table. It surfaces patterns; it does not give financial advice.

You can also ask follow-up questions about your own trading patterns. Every answer is grounded in your data — observations only, not recommendations.

Flash and Haiku are included. Gemini Pro, Claude Sonnet, and Claude Opus are part of the HappyTrader AI add-on.

What is the Strategy fit matrix?

A heatmap of every strategy you've used crossed against every sector you've traded. Each cell shows how your real trades performed — expectancy, win rate, and number of trades.

It surfaces sweet spots ("Covered Calls in Tech are paying off") and soft spots ("Cash-Secured Puts in Energy are bleeding"), so you can lean into what works and avoid the corners that don't.

How do you tag sectors and subsectors?

Every ticker you trade is automatically tagged with its sector and subsector using public market data — no manual entry. Your performance rolls up by sector on the Sectors page so you can see at a glance where your edge actually lives.
